Where your money goes

One child's place for a full year

Over a year, your gift pays for one child's place at the Education Center.

Help with school

Teaching in English, Mathematics and Science on the Punjab Board curriculum, alongside the government school the children attend by day.

Bible teaching

Religious Studies, including Bible teaching, Gospel learning, Christian identity, and discipleship, so each child can know Jesus for themselves.

Learning materials

Books and the learning materials a child needs to keep up in class and grow in confidence.

Questions

Questions donors ask

Can I give monthly, or just once?

Either. Give whatever amount works for you, monthly or once.

Does my gift go to one child?

Gifts go to the Center as a whole rather than to one named child. That way every child is cared for in the same way, whether or not they have a sponsor. If you would like to get to know one child, you can sponsor a child's place.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. Every gift is recorded, and we send a written receipt for each one.

Whose name is the bank account in?

The account is held in the name of our founder, Zuhaib Asgher, who has personally funded the ministry since 2024.

Can our church or group give together?

Yes, and we would love that. Please contact us and we will talk it through together.
