Our Mission

Educate. Train. Develop.

Universal Truth Ministry exists to educate children, train leaders, and develop communities through Christ-centered programs — including quality education, vocational and technical skills training, and community development. Rooted in the Christian community, we serve everyone in need across Pakistan's remote and neglected areas, following the teaching of Jesus.

Current Project

Universal Truth Education Center

Layyah, Pakistan

Our current project is the Universal Truth Education Center in Layyah, already serving 40+ Christian children from families with limited financial and educational resources, providing quality education and faith-based training in a remote and under-resourced community.

As we grow, our plans include a scholarship program, vocational training, foster care and child welfare support, and broader community development initiatives.

  • Quality, Christ-centered education
  • Vocational and technical skills training
  • Community development in Pakistan's remote areas
