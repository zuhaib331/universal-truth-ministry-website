Layyah, South Punjab, Pakistan

Helping Christian children in South Punjab learn, believe and lead.

Every afternoon, 35 children come to the Universal Truth Education Center for extra lessons and Bible teaching, with local Christian teachers who know every family by name.

Sponsor a child Give once See our work

“Let the little children come to me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of heaven belongs to such as these.”

Matthew 19:14

It began in our family home.

“My parents opened our house to this work in 2007. It stopped in 2016 when my father died. I returned in January 2024, reopened it, and formally established Universal Truth Ministry. Our teachers are from this community and know every family by name.”

Zuhaib AsgherFounder & Executive Director

Christian children in South Punjab are often the first left out of a good education.

1 in 3

girls in Pakistan is out of school

About one in three girls is not in school, compared with one in four boys.

Source: Household Integrated Economic Survey 2024–25, Pakistan Bureau of Statistics
52%

of women in Layyah can read

In our district, 71% of men can read, but only 52% of women. Every girl who learns changes that for the next generation.

Source: Pakistan Census 2023, Layyah District
