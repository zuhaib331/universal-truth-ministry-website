Pray with us for South Punjab

We believe prayer is foundational to everything we do. A few current requests for South Punjab:

For the children and families

  • Safety, health, and growth for every student at the Education Center in Layyah, South Punjab
  • Faith and spiritual growth in every child, not just their education
  • Provision and resilience for Christian families across South Punjab facing poverty and exclusion

For our team

  • Endurance and a sense of calling for the teachers serving these children
  • Strength and protection for the founder, who carries much of this work personally
  • A strong, like-minded team to help lead and grow the Ministry across South Punjab

For the Ministry

  • Provision for the daily costs of running the Education Center, since giving is still personal and informal
  • Favor and goodwill for the Ministry within local communities and with authorities across South Punjab
  • Wisdom on the right timing to grow this work to serve more of South Punjab

“Let the little children come to me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of heaven belongs to such as these.”

Matthew 19:14
