Privacy Policy

Universal Truth Ministry respects your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website or contacting us through it, you agree to this policy.

Who we are

Universal Truth Ministry is a faith-based organization based in Layyah, South Punjab, Pakistan. We run the Universal Truth Education Center, and we are responsible for the personal information collected through this website. You can reach us any time through our Contact page.

What information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us. When you use our contact form, or write to us by email or phone, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, the reason you are reaching out, and the content of your message. If you contact us about giving, we may also collect the details we need to arrange your gift.

Please do not send sensitive personal information (such as financial account numbers or health details) through the contact form.

How we use your information

We use your information only to reply to you and to carry on our work with you. For example, to answer a question, follow up on a volunteering or partnership request, respond to a prayer request, or share bank transfer details with someone who wants to give. We may keep a simple record of our correspondence. We do not use your information for any other purpose.

How your information is handled and stored

Our contact form is delivered to us by email through a trusted third-party service called Web3Forms. Your message is sent over a secure (HTTPS) connection and arrives in our ministry email inbox. We do not currently take card or online payments on this website, so we do not collect or store payment card details here. Any details needed to arrange a gift are handled privately and kept only as long as needed. If we add online giving in the future, we will update this policy to explain how payment information is handled.

Cookies and website analytics

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking tools to build a profile of you. This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may record your device's IP address as part of delivering those fonts. Our website host may also keep standard, short-term server logs (such as IP addresses and pages visited) to keep the site running safely. We do not use these to identify you personally.

When we share information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone. We only share it with the service providers that help us run this website and reply to you (such as our form service and our email and hosting providers), and only so they can do that job. We may also share information if the law requires us to.

Children's information

We take special care with children. We do not publish the full names, exact addresses, or identifying photos of the children we serve without permission from a parent or guardian, obtained in writing wherever possible. This website is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it. See our Child Safeguarding page for more detail.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information only as long as we need it to reply to you and to keep a basic record of our work. When it is no longer needed, we remove it. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also tell us at any time to stop sending you updates. To do any of these, just contact us and we will help.

Links to other websites

Our website links to other places, such as our LinkedIn page. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date below. We will also review and update it as Universal Truth Ministry completes its legal registration and as data-protection law in Pakistan develops.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or about your information, please reach us through our Contact page or by email at zuhaib.asgher@universaltruthministry.org.
