Current Project

Universal Truth Education Center

Layyah, South Punjab, Pakistan

Our current project is the Universal Truth Education Center in Layyah, running since January 2024 and already serving 35 Christian children from families with limited financial and educational resources — 15 boys and 20 girls, ages 5 to 16. The Center provides quality education and faith-based training in a remote and under-resourced community.

Students follow the Punjab Board curriculum in core subjects — English, Math, and Science — alongside Religious Studies, including Bible teaching, Gospel learning, Christian identity, and discipleship, because we care as much about who they're becoming as what they're learning.
