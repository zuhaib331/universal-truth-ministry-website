What We Do

Running now

Universal Truth Education Center

Layyah, South Punjab Since January 2024 35 children, ages 4 to 16 6 classes a week

Our current project is the Universal Truth Education Center in Layyah, running since January 2024 and already serving 35 Christian children from families with limited financial and educational resources (15 boys and 20 girls, ages 4 to 16). The Center provides quality education and faith-based training in a remote and under-resourced community.

The children attend government school by day and come to us in the afternoon. There, the same local Christian teachers help them with their school subjects and open the Bible with them, because we care as much about who they're becoming as what they're learning.

How a day works

One afternoon, the whole child

Lessons and faith are not two separate projects for us. They happen together, with the same teachers, in the same afternoon.

Mind

Extra lessons in English, Mathematics and Science on the Punjab Board curriculum.

Faith

Bible teaching, Gospel learning and discipleship, with daily Bible reading and a Bible test every Monday.

Care

Local Christian teachers who know every family by name, under our written child safeguarding policy.

Family

Our teachers meet parents regularly to support each child's growth at home and in class.
