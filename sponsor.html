Sponsor a Child

About US$38 a month (US$456 a year) gives one child a full year at the Universal Truth Education Center in Layyah: help with school, learning materials, and Bible teaching.

Meet the Children How It Works

Children Waiting for a Sponsor

Meet the children

These children come to the Education Center to learn and to read the Bible. Choose a child, and your gift will help them and every other child at the Center.

To protect the children, their photos and full stories open after you tell us your name and email.

Stories coming soon

We are preparing the first stories together with the children's parents. You can already sponsor a child's place today, and we will introduce you to a child as soon as their story is ready.

Sponsor a child's place

To keep every child safe, we use first names only (sometimes changed) and share photos only with written permission from a parent. Read our Child Safeguarding policy.

What Your Sponsorship Covers

One child's place for a full year

Help with school

Teaching in English, Mathematics and Science on the Punjab Board curriculum, alongside the government school the children attend by day.

Bible teaching

Religious Studies, including Bible teaching, Gospel learning, Christian identity, and discipleship, so each child can know Jesus for themselves.

Learning materials

Books and the learning materials a child needs to keep up in class and grow in confidence.

How It Works

Four simple steps

01

Choose a child

Read the stories and choose a child, or let us choose for you.

02

Tell us

Send the short form below, or message us on WhatsApp.

03

Give by bank transfer

Give monthly, yearly, or once. The bank details are on our Donate page, and we will send them to you too.

04

Stay connected

We send a written receipt for every gift, and news from the Center twice a year.

How your gift is used

Your gift supports the whole Education Center, so every child is helped, including the child you choose. This keeps things fair for every child, including the children whose stories are not online.

Questions

Questions people ask

How long does sponsorship last?

As long as you like. You can stop at any time. Just let us know.

Can I write to the child?

Yes. Send your letter or card to us, and we will share it with the child at the Center. To keep the family safe, we do not share home addresses.

What happens if the child leaves the Center?

We will let you know, and we can introduce you to another child who is waiting for a sponsor.

Can our church or group sponsor a whole class?

Yes, and we would love that. Please contact us and we will talk it through together.

Why does my gift go to the whole Center?

So that every child is cared for in the same way, whether or not they have a sponsor. The child you choose is helped, and so is every child learning beside them.
