# Universal Truth Ministry — Terminology Style Guide

Use these terms consistently across all organizational documents, presentations, website content,
fundraising materials, governance documents, policy manuals, and public communications. Replace the
old term directly — do not add explanations alongside it.

| Do not use | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Spiritual grooming | "Spiritual formation" or "Christian character development" (choose based on context) |
| Home-based school | "Home-based supplementary education and Bible-formation program" |
| Religious Studies | "Religious Studies, including Bible teaching, Gospel learning, Christian identity, and discipleship" |
| Underprivileged Christian children | "Christian children from families with limited financial and educational resources" |

## Notes

- These replacements apply to all future materials, not just the website.
- Approved: 2026-07-24.
- Applied so far: website (universal-truth-ministry.html) — "underprivileged students" updated in the
  "Our Work" section.
